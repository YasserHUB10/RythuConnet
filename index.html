The Problem

Rythu Bazaar, Mehdipatnam has a trust problem

Empathy research with 3 user interviews + geo-tagged field photos revealed three recurring pain points.

  • 🚗

    Parking Chaos

    The market's single entry point creates bottlenecks. Shoppers circle for minutes with no visibility into available slots. Raised in the Telangana State Assembly, September 2026.

    Pain point · Accessibility
  • 📋

    Price Board vs Stall Reality

    The government-posted price board is updated infrequently. Vendors charge different prices at the stall. Shoppers have no quick way to flag or report the discrepancy.

    Pain point · Transparency
  • ❓

    Who Is Actually a Registered Farmer?

    Quality varies and claims conflict — shoppers cannot tell a registered farmer-vendor from an unregistered trader. Trust in the farmer-direct model erodes without a quick-verify mechanism.

    Pain point · Trust

How It Works

Six steps, zero confusion

  1. 1

    Vendor Registers

    Farmer visits the Estate Officer window. Name, produce, and plot number recorded in the app.

  2. →
  3. 2

    Officer Issues QR ID

    System generates a unique QR card. Printed and laminated at the stall — no smartphone needed by the vendor.

  4. →
  5. 3

    Daily Price Synced

    Officer inputs today's government rates each morning. All app and SMS views update instantly.

  6. →
  7. 4

    Shopper Checks Price & Vendor

    Opens app to see today's board, or texts PRICE TOMATO via SMS. Scans QR to verify the farmer on the spot.

  8. →
  9. 5

    Parking Count Updated Live

    Attendant taps Enter / Leave (or IR sensor triggers), keeping the free-slot count accurate for incoming shoppers.

  10. →
  11. 6

    Feedback Logged

    Shoppers rate produce and vendors, and flag price discrepancies in one tap. Logs surface to the Estate Officer dashboard for follow-up.

Tech: Flutter Node.js + Firebase SMS / USSD Gateway QR Generation IR / Manual Counter

Pilot Plan

Starting at one market, done right

Single-market pilot at Mehdipatnam Rythu Bazaar. Estate-Officer-assisted onboarding. SMS fallback ensures no vendor is left behind.

🚀 Pilot Approach

  • Start with Mehdipatnam only — one officer, one market
  • Officer onboards vendors during existing registration window
  • QR cards printed on site (no vendor app install required)
  • SMS gateway covers vendors without smartphones
  • Parking counter starts with manual tap; IR sensor as upgrade

⚠️ Risks & Mitigations

Risk Mitigation
Officer adoption lag Simple one-screen UI; officer demo session on day 0
Low-bandwidth connectivity Offline-first cache; SMS as full fallback
QR card damage / loss Officer can reprint; ID lives in Firebase
Shopper awareness Board signage + loudspeaker announcement at gate
